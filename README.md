# Biashara Smart 🇰🇪

**Your business, made simple.**

Biashara Smart is a business management app designed to help everyday Kenyan entrepreneurs and small-business owners understand their money, track their transactions, and make better business decisions.

## 🎯 Our Mission

To make business record-keeping simple and accessible to every Kenyan entrepreneur, including those who have never used accounting software.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Money In:** Record sales and other income.
- **Money Out:** Track expenses such as stock, rent, transport, wages, and utilities.
- **Taken Home:** Track money withdrawn from the business for personal use.
- **Estimated Profit:** Understand how your business is performing.
- **Customer Debts:** Keep track of money customers owe you.
- **Business Dashboard:** Review business activity and financial summaries.
- **Monthly Reports:** Understand trends and identify opportunities to improve.
- **Business Health Score:** Help business owners identify areas that need attention.

## 👥 Who Is It For?

Biashara Smart is designed for small-business owners, informal traders, shopkeepers, salons, food vendors, and other entrepreneurs across Kenya.

## 🛠️ Project Status

Biashara Smart is currently in its early development stage. The project aims to grow into a practical business-management platform with secure data storage, reporting, and additional tools for entrepreneurs.

## 🌍 Vision

To empower millions of African entrepreneurs with simple tools that help them manage their businesses, build reliable financial records, and access opportunities for growth.

## 🚀 Getting Started

The initial version is a web app. Open `index.html` in a compatible web browser to explore it locally.

Online hosting, database integration, and additional features will be developed as the project progresses.
